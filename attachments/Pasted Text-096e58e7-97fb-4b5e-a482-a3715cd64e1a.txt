Use:

- Strong typography and clear visual hierarchy.
- A carefully selected color palette.
- Professional spacing, composition, and alignment.

Avoid generic layouts, excessive gradients, unnecessary glass effects, overcrowded sections, excessive animation, and decorative elements that add no value.

The design should be recognizable and memorable without compromising readability or usability.

Ensure the website works properly on mobile phones, tablets, laptops, and large desktop screens.
